Getting there

From Larnaca or Nicosia

Motorway A1 towards Limassol, exit Agios Tychonas, follow signs to the resort. About 70 km, 40–50 minutes.

From Paphos

A6 towards Limassol, exit Agios Tychonas. About 70 km, 50 minutes.

By bus from Limassol centre

EMEL route 30 eastbound to Amathountos Ave. – Hawaii Hotel, then a 2-minute walk. About 20 minutes.

By car or bike from the centre

Follow the coastal road east for about 6 km (15–20 minutes). The venue connects to Limassol’s bicycle lanes.
