About

I am passionate about using technology to support, improve and transform processes and activities. I keep a close eye on industry standards, emerging trends and challenges, particularly in usability, accessibility, user-centred design and emerging technologies, and I bring diverse international experience to that work.

I believe technology is a powerful enabler of lifelong learning, continuous development and improved productivity across disciplines. My work explores how technology can be designed and applied meaningfully to enhance human experiences, support learning and improve the way people work.
